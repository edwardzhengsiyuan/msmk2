News Release | Momentis Biotech's Four Innovative Products "Hengdawei", "Zhongke Sigu", "Vike® Hearing", and "Keyan Professor" Grandly Launched in Shenzhen
2026年1月29日，深圳 – 今日，每时每刻生物科技（深圳）有限公司（Momentis Biotech (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.） 在深圳市南山区科兴科学园会议中心成功举办“创新驱动，守护未来”新产品发布会，集中发布并隆重介绍了四大战略性创新产品：新一代SGLT2抑制剂恒达维（Bexagliflozin/BRENZAVVY™）、中科硅骨可吸收人工骨粉、太尔骨听骨导式助听器及科妍教授汉麻纤维卫生巾。活动吸引了近200位嘉宾现场参与，其中包括来自全国各地的顶级临床专家、科研领军人物、行业领导及重要合作伙伴，共同见证了这一重要时刻。
January 29, 2026, Shenzhen – Momentis Biotech (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. successfully held the "Innovation Driven, Safeguarding the Future" new product launch event at the Kexing Science Park Conference Center in Nanshan District, Shenzhen. The event featured the grand introduction of four strategic innovative products: the latest-generation SGLT2 inhibitor Hengdawei (Bexagliflozin/BRENZAVVY™), Zhongke Sigu Artificial Absorbable Bone Powder, Vike® Bone Conduction Hearing Aid, and Keyan Professor Hempep Napkin. The event attracted nearly 200 guests, including top clinical experts, leading scientific researchers, industry leaders, and key partners from across the country, all gathered to witness this significant milestone.
四大创新成果，引领行业突破
Four Innovative Achievements Leading Industry Breakthroughs
本次发布的四款产品分别针对糖尿病合并肾病、骨缺损修复、听力健康及女性个人护理领域，均代表了各自领域的重大技术突破与临床价值。
The four launched products target diabetes with chronic kidney disease, bone defect repair, hearing health, and feminine personal care, respectively, each representing a major technological breakthrough and significant clinical value in its field.
恒达维 (Bexagliflozin/BRENZAVVY™): 作为新一代SGLT2抑制剂，恒达维拥有超过1500例、长达3.5年的心血管安全性数据。它是目前唯一经临床试验验证，适用于肾小球滤过率（eGFR）在30-45 mL/min的糖尿病合并慢性肾病3b期患者的药物。该产品不仅填补了高危人群的临床治疗空白，其延缓肾功能衰竭进展、降低心血管事件风险的系统价值，更能为医保基金节省长期透析与大额住院支出，是一款兼具临床突破与经济价值的“节省型创新药”。
中科硅骨可吸收人工骨粉: 该产品是松山湖材料实验室科研成果产业化的成果，并已获得国内首款硫酸钙人工骨三类医疗器械注册证。其采用自主晶型调控技术，纯度超99%，可实现骨愈合与降解同步（2-3个月完全吸收），并具备高达50MPa的机械强度和长达5周的持续抗菌载药能力，为感染性及难愈性骨缺损提供了从结构修复到抗感染治疗的一体化国产创新解决方案。
太尔骨听骨导式助听器 (Vike® Hearing Aid): 搭载革命性软髁突振子与髁突传声技术，通过专利设计将适用人群从传导性耳聋扩展至主流的轻、中度神经性及混合性耳聋。产品采用开放耳道佩戴设计，舒适卫生，具备双耳自动降噪、防啸叫、长续航（日常使用7天）等特点，为更广泛的听损人群提供了新一代智慧听力解决方案。

科妍教授汉麻纤维卫生巾 (Keyan Professor Hempep Napkin): 采用天然汉麻纤维及三维抑菌技术，实现物理阻隔与植物源抑菌的双重防护。产品天然抑菌防臭、吸湿透气，面料柔软亲肤、低刺激，同时其原料种植节水、产品易降解，融合了天然健康与环保可持续的理念。
Hengdawei (Bexagliflozin/BRENZAVVY™): As the latest-generation SGLT2 inhibitor, Hengdawei is backed by long-term cardiovascular safety data from over 1,500 subjects across 3.5 years. It is the only agent clinically validated for patients with diabetes complicated by chronic kidney disease (CKD) stage 3b with an estimated glomerular filtration rate (eGFR) of 30-45 mL/min. This product not only fills the clinical treatment gap for this high-risk population but also delivers systemic value by delaying renal failure progression and reducing cardiovascular event risks. It helps save long-term dialysis and high-cost hospitalization expenditures, positioning it as a cost-saving innovative drug.
Zhongke Sigu Artificial Absorbable Bone Powder: This product is industrialized from the scientific research achievements of Songshan Lake Materials Laboratory and has obtained China’s first Class III medical device registration certificate for calcium sulfate artificial bone. Using independently controllable crystal form regulation technology with purity exceeding 99%, it achieves synchronized bone healing and degradation (complete absorption within 2-3 months). With a mechanical strength of up to 50 MPa and sustained antibacterial drug-loading for up to 5 weeks, it provides an integrated domestic innovative solution for infectious and refractory bone defects.
Vike® Bone Conduction Hearing Aid: Equipped with revolutionary soft condylar oscillator and condylar sound transmission technology, its patented design expands the target population from conductive hearing loss to the mainstream mild-to-moderate sensorineural and mixed hearing loss. Featuring an open-ear, non-insertable wearing design, it is comfortable and hygienic, with functions like automatic binaural noise reduction, anti-howling, and long battery life (7 days for daily use), offering a new-generation intelligent hearing solution for a wider audience with hearing impairment.
Keyan Professor Hempep Napkin: Made of natural hemp fiber and utilizing 3D antibacterial technology, it provides dual protection through physical barrier and plant-sourced antibacterial effects. The product offers natural antibacterial and odor prevention, moisture absorption, and breathability. Its soft, skin-friendly surface is low-irritation. With water-efficient cultivation of raw materials and ready biodegradability, it combines concepts of natural wellness and environmental sustainability.
权威专家云集，共话产品价值与未来
Gathering of Authoritative Experts Discussing Product Value and Future
发布会特邀了与四大产品领域相关的多位顶尖专家进行主题分享与讨论，深度解读了产品的临床意义、技术优势与应用前景。
The launch event invited several top experts in the fields related to the four products for thematic presentations and discussions, providing in-depth interpretation of the products' clinical significance, technological advantages, and application prospects.
在医药经济与内分泌领域，暨南大学南方药物经济学研究所所长蒋杰教授、深圳市第二人民医院内分泌科主任医师阎德文博士及南方科技大学医院内分泌科主任李元宾博士等专家，从药物经济学、真实世界研究及临床实践角度，深入探讨了以恒达维为代表的新型降糖药在慢病管理中的综合价值。
在骨科材料领域，东莞市人民医院骨科学科主任黎松波教授结合临床需求，阐释了创新骨修复材料对治疗的重要意义。中国科学院化学研究所研究员邱东博士与松山湖材料实验室团队负责人方灿良博士则从材料科学前沿角度，剖析了中科硅骨产品的技术核心与产业化突破。
在听力健康与中医领域，山东中医药大学中医耳鼻喉学科带头人王仁忠教授分享了中西医结合在耳部健康方面的见解。哈佛大学医学院博士后、副主任医师何泓副教授也从跨学科视角交流了听力干预的最新趋势。
In the fields of pharmacoeconomics and endocrinology, experts including Professor Jiang Jie, Director of the Southern Institute of Pharmacoeconomics at Jinan University; Dr. Yan Dewen, Chief Physician of the Endocrinology Department at Shenzhen Second People's Hospital; and Dr. Li Yuanbin, Director of the Endocrinology Department at Southern University of Science and Technology Hospital discussed the comprehensive value of new diabetes drugs like Hengdawei in chronic disease management from perspectives of pharmacoeconomics, real-world research, and clinical practice.
In the field of orthopedic biomaterials, Professor Li Songbo, Director of the Orthopedics Department at Dongguan People's Hospital, explained the significance of innovative bone repair materials based on clinical needs. Dr. Qiu Dong, Researcher at the Institute of Chemistry, Chinese Academy of Sciences, and Dr. Fang Canliang, Team Leader at the Songshan Lake Materials Laboratory, analyzed the technological core and industrialization breakthrough of the Zhongke Sigu product from the forefront of materials science.
In hearing health and traditional Chinese medicine, Professor Wang Renzhong, leading expert in ENT of TCM at Shandong University of Traditional Chinese Medicine, shared insights on integrative medicine for ear health. Associate Professor He Hong, Deputy Chief Physician and Postdoctoral Fellow from Harvard Medical School, also exchanged views on the latest trends in hearing intervention from an interdisciplinary perspective.
