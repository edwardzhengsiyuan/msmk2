每时健康（深圳）有限公司


关于我们
每时健康科技有限公司是每时每刻生物科技有限公
司旗下的全资子公司，承载着将创新健康科技服务于日常生活的战略使命。



业务
公司专注于运营“每时每刻”品牌旗下的大健康消费产品线，致力于通过前沿科技与人性化设计，为消费者提供即时、便捷、高效的健康防护与生活护理解决方案。



使命
秉持"通过持续创新，服务健康美好生活”的企业使命，一切工作以提升用户健康福祉为出发点。


历史与构成
公司依托母公司每时每刻生物科技多年深厚的大健康与生物医药行业积淀，于 2025 年正式成立，自诞生之初便具备成熟的研发支撑、供应链体系与行业资源；成立后快速完成线下销售、国内电商运营、海外电商运营三大核心营销团队搭建，同步整合国内 60 万家药房、头部 CS/KA/CVS 连锁等线下渠道资源，以及京东、天猫、抖音等线上平台资源，实现国内全渠道布局的快速落地。



企业构成
公司内部搭建 “核心营销 + 综合支撑” 的组织架构，核心设线下销售部、国内电商运营部、海外电商运营部三大业务部门，同时配备市场部、供应链部、品控部、综合管理部等支撑部门，保障全渠道运营的高效推进；外部依托母公司研发中心、市场医学中心，以及海南昊天健康科技有限公司核心销售网络，实现研发、生产、销售的全方位协同，为公司发展提供强大的资源与能力支撑




愿景
以“成为全球领先的全周期健康科技服务商”为愿景，着眼于长远发展，不断拓展健康服务的边界。



使命
聚焦消费者日常健康护理的核心需求与未被满足的消费痛点，依托集团强大的研发实力与全球化资源，通过持续的产品创新与全渠道运营，打造兼具科技性与实用性的大健康消费产品；以 “让健康融入生活中的每时每刻” 为初心，坚守品质底线，深耕国内市场，拓展全球市场，为消费者提供即时、便捷、高效的健康解决方案，为人类健康福祉贡献 “每时每刻” 的力量。


规模
公司汇聚了一支由大健康运营、电商运营、跨境电商、市场营销、供应链管理等领域资深专家组成的专业团队，核心管理层与业务骨干均拥有 10 年以上行业经验，其中核心管理层具备 20 + 年医药健康领域操盘经验，对大健康行业发展趋势、市场运营逻辑拥有深刻理解与丰富实战经验。

公司已在国内主要城市建立线下渠道运营网络，合作覆盖全国 60 万家 OTC 药房、数万家 CS/KA/CVS 连锁门店，国内电商平台实现主流渠道全覆盖，海外业务已辐射美国、英国、东南亚、巴西等多个国家和地区；依托母公司成熟的供应链管理与合规体系，搭建了高效的产品交付与品质管控体系，同时与国内外数十家头部企业建立战略合作伙伴关系，形成了 “产品 + 渠道 + 资源” 的规模化发展格局，团队规模随业务发展持续稳步扩张，成为大健康消费领域极具发展潜力的专业运营团队。

About Us

Vision
With the vision of "Becoming a globally leading full-cycle health technology service provider," the company focuses on long-term development, continuously expanding the boundaries of health services.

MissionFocus on the core needs and unmet consumption pain points of consumers' daily health care, rely on the group's strong R&D strength and global resources, create big health consumer products with both technology and practicality through continuous product innovation and omni-channel operation; with the original aspiration of "integrating health into every moment of life", adhere to the quality bottom line, deeply cultivate the domestic market, expand the global market, provide consumers with instant, convenient and efficient health solutions, and contribute "Momentis" strength to human health and well-being.


Scale
The company has assembled a professional team of seasoned experts in fields such as health and wellness operations, e-commerce operations, cross-border e-commerce, marketing, and supply chain management. The core management team and key business backbones all possess over 10 years of industry experience, with core management having 20+ years of experience in the pharmaceutical and health sector. They hold deep insights into industry development trends and market operation logic, complemented by extensive practical experience.


The company has established an offline channel operation network across major domestic cities, collaborating with 600,000 OTC pharmacies and tens of thousands of CS/KA/CVS chain stores nationwide. It has achieved full coverage of mainstream domestic e-commerce platforms, with overseas business now reaching multiple countries and regions including the United States, the United Kingdom, Southeast Asia, and Brazil. Leveraging the parent company's mature supply chain management and quality control systems, it has built an efficient product delivery and quality assurance system. Simultaneously, it has established strategic partnerships with dozens of leading domestic and international enterprises, forming a large-scale development pattern of "Products + Channels + Resources." The team size continues to expand steadily alongside business growth, establishing itself as a professional operations team with significant development potential in the health and wellness consumer sector.

愿景：成为立足中国、链接全球的创新型生物医药企业，让前沿医疗成果惠及更多生命。

使命：聚焦未被满足的临床需求，通过精准的国际合作与专业化的本土运营，将海外优质的原研药物引入中国，并辐射更广阔的海外市场，为人类健康福祉贡献“每时每刻”的力量。


构成：
下设研发中心、市场医学中心，并由海南昊天健康科技有限公司作为核心销售中心。

规模：
公司汇聚了一支由医药研发、注册事务、市场营销及国际贸易等领域专家组成的多元化团队，在中国主要城市及海外关键市场建立了高效的业务网络。凭借稳健的供应链管理与合规体系，每时每刻正加速成为国际医药合作领域中值得信赖的践行者。

海南昊天健康科技有限公司


关于我们
海南昊天健康科技有限公司成立于1994年，坐落于海南自贸港海南生态软件园区，是集药品、医疗器械、大健康产品等营销为一体的GSP现代化综合销售企业。


业务：
聚焦妇产科、肿瘤科、内分泌科、眼科、肾科、耳鼻喉科等核心疾病领域。后续拓展为由药品、器械、健康服务三大板块相互联动、循环支撑，共同构成一个完整的产业或服务生态系统。



历史与构成：
  海南昊天健康科技有限公司成立于1994年，原为上市公司翰宇药业全资子公司，坐落于海南自贸港海南生态软件园区，是集药品、医疗器械、大健康产品等营销为一体的GSP现代化综合销售企业，2025年被深圳星源生物医药投资公司并购为全资子公司。



愿景：
成为中国领先的、基于海南自贸港优势的医药健康产业综合服务平台，让创新医疗成果惠及每一位中国患者。


使命：
链接全球医药创新，服务中国健康需求，成为值得信赖的全生命周期健康解决方案提供者。


构成：
公司业务分为三大产品线。药品线下设药品线产品经理、华东、华西、华北、华南、华中五大区销售总监及省区经理。助听器产品线下设助听器产品经理及片区招商总监。人工骨粉产品线下设人工骨粉产品经理及片区招商总监。

规模：
公司汇聚了深耕医药行业多年的专业人员，深度熟悉中国药品上市与注册全流程，可高效推进产品合规落地。精准把握国谈、集采等国家医药政策，并具备成熟的应对方案，能助力企业在复杂政策环境中抢占先机。团队深耕中国医药市场，曾引进多家跨国公司企业产品，具备丰富的市场运作经验。专业营销团队规模超 100 人，可高效支撑产品推广与市场拓展。

福建太尔集团股份有限公司
福建太尔集团股份有限公司成立于2010年，是一家专注于骨传导技术及听力健康领域的国家高新技术企业。公司早期作为三星等国际品牌的代工厂，积累了深厚的声学制造经验，并于2014年在新三板挂牌上市（股票代码：830886），成为中国国内骨传导声学领域首家上市企业。
太尔集团以改变人类听音方式为愿景，致力于将先进的产品和优质的服务带给用户。从创立至今，公司一直践行“引领变革，追求一流，心怀天下，品质示人”的企业理念，将工 匠精神渗入每件产品的每道工序，实践商业向善，展现科技 温情。
未来，公司将继续围绕用户健康需求持续创新，为构建人类 命运共同体贡献科技力量。
业务
太尔集团对骨传导这一神奇现象进行了深入的探索研究，在声音的编码解码、降噪滤波、声波敏感采集、不同介质间的稳定性传输及相关材料创新应用等方面都取得了巨大突破。
随着移动互联网及智能化设备的普及，太尔集团又在骨传导技术的基础上进行设备微型化、时尚化的研究，并设法大幅降低成本，使其满足社会大众的使用及购买需求，开发出一系列以骨传导为核心的智能健康产品，开辟了听力智能健康领域一个全新的方向，通过结合骨传导助听设备和听力健康领域专家的服务，为患者提供听力健康整体解决方案。
历史
2010 年，福建太尔电子科技有限公司成立，曾为三星手机/耳机/扬声器等配件的主要代工厂。
2012 年，开始研发骨导式助听器。
2014 年，太尔科技在新三板挂牌上市。
2015 年完成初步骨传导式助听器的相关研发成果并开始临床试验。
2015 年，VLIKE 微籁健康品牌正式发布，富士康全资子公司签署系列合作协议，漳州片仔癀药业股份有限公司成为战略投资人。
2015 年至2019年1月，全球首款骨导式助听器完成临床试验，获得第一款产品的国家准字号及医疗器械许可证，并在全国范围内进行电视宣传，同时开设667 家VLIKE 线下专卖店，当年营收超过4000万元。
2018年，企业正式更名为福建太尔集团股份有限公司
竞争与挑战
2020年，公司遭受六大国际品牌的打压，使公司陷入官司纠纷，导致公司吊销生产许可证，并停止生产近4年。
2023年10 月，经过3年10个月的案件审理，公司因说明书问题被罚款400万元。尽管面临困难，公司依然坚持运营，未解散。
重组与复苏
公司面临一些负债，土地被查封并已法拍，但通过3000万元以内可以解决所有负债。
2023年10月，公司重新获得产品许可，于2024年恢复生产及运营，并实现盈利。
2024年，公司战略调整，与国大药房等多家药房合作，整合线上线下营销渠道，计划五年内由集团主体重新在主板上市。
2026年1月，每时每刻生物科技（深圳）有限公司以最大股东公身份控股太尔集团，太尔集团成为每时每刻重要战略伙伴之一。

Fujian Taier Group Co.,Ltd.


Fujian Taier Group Co., Ltd. , founded in 2010, is a national high-tech enterprise specializing in bone conduction technology and hearing health solutions. Initially serving as an OEM manufacturer for international brands such as Samsung, the company accumulated extensive expertise in acoustic engineering. In 2014, it was listed on the NEEQ (New Third Board, Stock Code: 830886), becoming the first publicly traded company in China's bone conduction acoustic sector.
Driven by the vision of transforming the way humans perceive sound, Taier Group is dedicated to delivering cutting-edge products and premium services to users. Since its inception, the company has consistently upheld its corporate philosophy—"Pioneering Change, Striving for Excellence, Embracing a Global Vision, Demonstrating Quality" —by embedding the spirit of craftsmanship into every step of production. In doing so, it practices "goodness in business" and showcases the human side of technology.
Looking ahead, the company will continue to innovate around user health needs, contributing technological strength to the building of a shared future for mankind.
The Taier Group has conducted in-depth exploration and research into the remarkable phenomenon of bone conduction, achieving significant breakthroughs in areas such as sound encoding and decoding, noise reduction filtering, sensitive acoustic wave capture, stable transmission across different media, and innovative applications of related materials.
With the proliferation of mobile internet and smart devices, Taier Group has further pursued research into device miniaturization and fashionable design based on bone conduction technology. By striving to substantially reduce costs, the company has made its products accessible and affordable for the general public. This has led to the development of a series of smart health products centered around bone conduction, opening up an entirely new direction in the field of hearing health technology. By integrating bone conduction hearing devices with the expertise of hearing health specialists, Taier Group provides patients with comprehensive hearing health solutions.
History
- In 2010, Fujian Taier Electronics Technology Co., Ltd. was established, initially serving as a major OEM manufacturer for Samsung mobile phone accessories, including headphones and speakers.
- In 2012, the company began research and development of bone conduction hearing aids.
- In 2014, Taier Technology was listed on the NEEQ (New Third Board).
- In 2015, preliminary R&D成果 for bone conduction hearing aids were completed, and clinical trials commenced.
- Also in 2015, the VLIKE smart health brand was officially launched. Foxconn's wholly-owned subsidiary signed a series of cooperation agreements, and Zhangzhou Pien Tze Huang Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. became a strategic investor.
- From 2015 to January 2019, the world's first bone conduction hearing aid completed clinical trials, obtained the first National Medical Device Registration Certificate and medical device license for its products, and launched nationwide television promotions. During this period, the company opened 667 VLIKE offline专卖店, achieving annual revenues exceeding 40 million RMB.
- In 2018, the company was officially renamed Fujian Taier Group Co., Ltd.**
Competition and Challenges
- In 2020, the company faced intense competition and legal challenges from six major international brands, leading to litigation. As a result, its production license was revoked, and operations ceased for nearly four years.
- In October 2023, after three years and ten months of legal proceedings, the company was fined 4 million RMB due to labeling issues. Despite these difficulties, the company persevered and remained intact.
Restructuring and Recovery
- The company incurred certain liabilities, and its land was seized and auctioned off through judicial proceedings. However, all outstanding debts can be settled within 30 million RMB.
- In October 2023, the company regained its product license. By 2024, it resumed production and operations and returned to profitability.
- In 2024, the company underwent strategic adjustments, partnering with China National Pharmacy and other major pharmacy chains, integrating online and offline marketing channels. It plans to return to the main board for listing under the group entity within five years.
- In January 2026, Momentis Biotech (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. assumed the position of the largest shareholder in Taier Group, which subsequently became one of Momentis's important strategic partners.
